# Example-status policy

The example directories contain either a corrected illustrative model or an
archived design sketch. They are not evidence of a completed gate unless they
link to a matching entry in [docs/RESULT_LEDGER.md](../docs/RESULT_LEDGER.md)
and executable result artifact.

The frozen evidence layer is intentionally narrower than the exploratory
design layer. Do not infer a theorem, a sample-complexity curve, or an
architecture result from an illustrative example.
